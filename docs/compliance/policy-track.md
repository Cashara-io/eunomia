# Separate policy and organisational track

This is the section 8 work register from the compliance-readiness plan, not a set of legal policies. Role owners below still need named people, approval and operating evidence. No placeholder is Done until the organisation adopts, uses and reviews the resulting document. These items are excluded from the 36 product-capability counts.

| Placeholder | Accountable role to assign | Separate deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| [Security and confidentiality programme](templates/security-programme.md) | Cashara security owner and each customer operator | Scope, objectives, risk ownership, access, classification and retention. |
| [Privacy notice and complaints procedure](templates/privacy-notice.md) | Cashara privacy owner for its collection, customer privacy owner for its installation | Review actual collection, rights, complaints and APP2/7/8/9 applicability. |
| [DPA template](templates/dpa.md) | Cashara legal owner with counsel | Assess actual processor activity in support or future hosting before drafting an Article 28 agreement. |
| [Incident response and notification runbooks](templates/incident-response.md) | Customer incident owner and Cashara product/security owner | Rehearse distinct NDB, GDPR and applicable AI decisions, evidence preservation and escalation. |
| [Business continuity and recovery](templates/continuity.md) | Customer operations owner with Cashara product support | Agree recovery objectives, key escrow, isolated copies and measured restore exercises. |
| [Secure development and vulnerability disclosure](templates/secure-development.md) | Cashara engineering/security owner | Review threat models, vulnerability intake, change review, licences, signing keys and patch deadlines. |
| [AI policy and terms of use](templates/ai-policy.md) | Cashara AI owner with legal review; customer use owner | Review intended purpose, Article 5 prohibited uses, limits, oversight and high-risk escalation. |
| [AI literacy and competence programme](templates/ai-literacy.md) | Cashara AI owner and customer training owner | Keep role-appropriate material versions, attendance, review and lessons from incidents. |
| [AI system lifecycle and monitoring](templates/ai-lifecycle.md) | Cashara AI owner and customer system owner | Define assessment approval, release criteria, oversight, monitoring, withdrawal and corrective action. |
| [Vendor and supply-chain management](templates/vendors.md) | Cashara procurement/security owner and customer infrastructure owner | Assess code, CI/release, support and actual non-model suppliers. Model-provider processing checks remain excluded. |
| [Evidence operation and independent assurance](templates/evidence-operation.md) | Cashara assurance owner and customer evidence owner | Retain scoped operating records, management decisions and independent assessment plans. |
